ROWAN JEVAN PORTFOLIO, VERSION 29

Current published website: black and white styling, animated stock simulation, and Wakefield > Dotmatics > Minerva under Los Angeles.

UPLOAD TO GITHUB
1. Unzip this file.
2. Open rowanjevan/rowanjevan.github.io on GitHub.
3. Choose Add file > Upload files.
4. Upload the extracted files and assets folder directly into the repository root. Do not upload the ZIP or an extra enclosing folder.
5. Commit the changes. Existing matching files will be replaced.
6. Keep your current Settings > Pages configuration, main branch and root folder.
7. After deployment finishes, refresh https://rowanjevan.github.io.

These files are the frontend. Keep the stock simulator backend on Render unchanged. Do not upload these files to the rowan-stock-simulator repository.

The frontend is connected to https://rowan-stock-simulator.onrender.com/api through ticker-config.js.

Alternative slogans discussed in chat have not been applied.
